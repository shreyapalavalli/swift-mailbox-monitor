The problem being solved: SGNY's shared SWIFT mailbox, which receives SWIFT messages sent to SGNY as a correspondent bank, is monitored by hand all day. It gets 200+ SWIFT messages a day (3,000+ a month): cancellations, amendments, callback requests and ABA (routing number) follow-ups, all arriving under the same standard subject line. Analysts have to open every email, find the message type, reference and request, then route, mark read, reply or escalate it. This is repetitive and error-prone, and it carries real risk: an urgent same-day amendment can get buried, and growing volumes make processing delays more likely.                                                                                                 Our proposed solution: SWIFT Mailbox Monitor connects to the Outlook mailbox through Microsoft Graph an checks it continuously. It automatically reads, understands, classifies, prioritises and actions every  SWIFT message, MT or MX:                                                                                - It extracts the message type, payment reference, related reference, sender and receiver bank codes   (BICs), amount and business purpose. It does this even from messy OCR'd text.                        
  - It applies the business rules automatically:
    - SGU references are routed to the CST team.
    - Cancellations are auto-closed and marked read, except camt.056, which goes to an analyst.
    - Amendments become priority alerts.
    - Callbacks with FT/INV references get an automatic acknowledgement.
    - ABA requests and exceptions are queued for analysts.
  - A real-time dashboard shows priority alerts, a work queue, the extracted key details, the reason behind each classification, and a full audit log.
- Target users:
  - SGNY Payments Operations analysts, who monitor the SWIFT mailbox day to day.
  - Operations team leads and supervisors, who need visibility of queue size, priorities and failures.
  - The CST team, who receive their SGU items automatically.
  - Control and audit functions, who need a traceable record of every message and every action taken.
- Expected business value:
  - It removes most of the manual open-read-classify work on 200+ daily messages. As an illustration, at about 2 minutes per message that's roughly 6–7 analyst-hours a day freed for genuine exceptions.
  - It catches urgent amendments within seconds instead of whenever someone gets to them, which reduces the risk of missed same-day actions and processing delays.
  - It strengthens controls: rules are applied consistently, with a complete audit trail and no reliance on individual judgement for routine items.
  - It improves customer service through faster acknowledgement of callbacks and quicker turnaround on amendments.
  - It scales with growing message volume without adding headcount.
- Key differentiators:
  - Explainable by design: every classification shows why, through the matched phrases and the rule applied. There's no black-box AI, so it's easy for auditors and risk teams to accept.
  - Standard-library NLP only: OCR clean-up, fuzzy matching for typos and negation handling ("do NOT cancel") without third-party ML packages, so there's nothing extra to get through a bank's security review.
  - Built to be safe on a live mailbox: each step is saved as it completes, so the system never forwards or replies twice, even after a crash. It also has a dry-run mode for safe rollout and keeps working through Microsoft sign-in expiry.
  - Real time: about 10–20 seconds from an email landing to an alert on the analyst's screen, with no page refresh.
  - Configurable rules, for example which message types always need an analyst and where CST items go, without code changes.
  - Proven quality: 427 automated backend tests plus browser tests of the full dashboard.
- Expected impact:
  - Analysts move from monitoring the mailbox to handling the exceptions. Most routine traffic, such as CST routing, cancellations and callback acknowledgements, is handled with no human touch.
  - Priority amendments are surfaced instantly with the key details needed to act.
  - Fewer missed or late actions, consistent rule application and full traceability.
  - A clear path to production: Graph webhooks, a managed database and single sign-on, plus the option to apply the same pattern to other shared operations mailboxes across the bank.
